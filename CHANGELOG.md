## 0.1.0

The first public release of **Photo Lifeboat**, free software that gets deleted photos back from memory cards.

- Rescues deleted photos, and videos, documents, music and other files too, from SD and other memory cards,
  including cards that have been formatted. Works with FAT and exFAT cards.
- Puts back together photos that the camera stored in scattered pieces, which most free tools can't, and checks
  each photo against the small preview the camera saved inside it.
- Never writes to your card: it makes a copy on your computer and works only from that.
- Simple step-by-step screens in a window of its own, in the Windows 11 style, light or dark.
- You choose where rescued files go: your Pictures folder, your Desktop, or a folder of your choice. Files are
  sorted into Photos, Videos, Documents, Music and sound, and Other files, with a plain-English report.
- When it's finished, it offers to delete the copy of the card to free up space, and explains why the card
  itself is best left alone for now.
- A proper installer (Start menu, desktop icon, uninstall from Settings > Apps, no administrator needed), or a
  portable zip if you'd rather not install anything.
- Windows 10 and 11. Mac and Linux versions are planned.
